# Scientific validation protocol

## Available data

The project initially contained no saved history for a 250-generation training run. Do not interpret the short diagnostic episode below as a trained-controller result. Run `npm run validate:training` to produce three complete runs, a per-generation CSV, the best networks, and paired controller comparisons in `results/`.

## Evaluation design

- Three independent seeds: 2026, 2027, and 2028 by default.
- 250 generations and 16 networks per generation; every network completes one physical episode before generation statistics are emitted.
- The same spawn randomization is used for all individuals within a run, so fitness differences within that run are not caused by different start positions. Different run seeds change network initialization, mutation sequence, and spawn.
- Episodes use the actual Catmull-Rom track boundaries and fixed 1/30 s physics, without a renderer.
- A lap requires a forward start-line crossing and at least 95% of the signed track length since the prior accepted lap. Progress is unwrapped along the closed path; reverse progress subtracts from the lap distance.
- Paired Steering/Neural comparisons use the same seed, segment, spawn interpolation, heading, geometry, timestep, termination rules, and reward calculator. Three seeds are compared.
- The CSV records generation best, global best, population mean, completed laps, signed progress, traveled track distance, episode time, speeds, off-track events/duration, blocked time, lateral error, and termination reasons.

## Reward audit

Reward is based on speed-bounded signed progress, validated laps, off-track and blocked penalties, and steering changes/reversals. Positive on-track and forward-speed bonuses now require positive validated progress for that step; this prevents stationary uptime or equal forward/reverse oscillation from accumulating a positive survival reward. A regression test covers a forward/backward loop. The lap threshold was raised from 80% to 95% after finding that random starts could otherwise count the first partial trip to the start line as a lap.

This remains a single-track, three-seed experiment. It estimates variability across seeds but does not establish generalization to other circuits or prove statistical significance. Report the generated results with the exact seed list and do not claim performance from reward alone; inspect laps, off-track time, blocking, and paired comparisons too.

## Diagnostic before full training

One deterministic, randomly initialized network (network seed 9, environment seed 2026) was physically evaluated during profiling. It stopped off-track after 263 steps (8.77 simulated seconds), with zero completed laps, 206.52 m signed progress, 3.57 s off-track, 11.61 m mean lateral error, and reward 193.80. This is one pre-training diagnostic under the then-current reward/lap settings; it is not part of the corrected three-seed run and must not be mixed into the final dataset.

## Observed results (2026-11-01)

The checked-in result artifact was generated by the protocol above: 250 generations × 16 physical episodes × 3 seeds = 12,000 training evaluations. No training episode was skipped. The best reward is the actual episode reward of the best retained network; the generation mean is the arithmetic mean of all 16 physical episode rewards in that generation. Re-running each saved best network under its original seed reproduced its saved best reward exactly.

| Seed | Best reward (generation) | Generation 250 mean reward | Laps completed by generation 250 population | Best episode outcome |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 2026 | 1107.61 (206) | 631.88 | 4 across 16 episodes | Objective; 1 validated lap; 0 off-track events; 0 blocked seconds; 1.72 m mean lateral error |
| 2027 | 1107.76 (173) | 806.21 | 5 across 16 episodes | Objective; 1 validated lap; 0 off-track events; 0 blocked seconds; 2.54 m mean lateral error |
| 2028 | 1108.04 (210) | 828.16 | 5 across 16 episodes | Objective; 1 validated lap; 0 off-track events; 0 blocked seconds; 0.89 m mean lateral error |

Across the three best rewards, the mean was 1107.80 and sample standard deviation was 0.22. This describes only three seeds on this track and fitness function. The average population in generation 250 was much lower than its best individual, so the best score should not be read as typical population behavior. Across all 12,000 episodes, termination reasons were 2,914 objective, 4,017 off-track, 223 blocked, and 4,846 time-limit. The off-track and blocked outcomes are frequent enough that the score alone does not establish robust driving.

The paired test used the same spawn segment, spawn interpolation, heading and timestep for both controllers at each seed. All six episodes completed one validated lap. Across the three comparisons, SteeringController averaged reward 1094.70, 2 off-track events and 0.99 s off-track, with 3.19 m mean lateral error. The saved NeuralControllers averaged reward 1107.80, zero off-track events and 0 s off-track, with 1.71 m mean lateral error. Mean signed progress was 919.37 m for Steering and 919.49 m for Neural; the track length is about 491.16 m. These are three paired runs, with no inference of statistical significance.

The approximately 919 m progress reported for an episode with one completed lap is consistent with the lap guard: an episode begins at a random point, and the first start-line crossing is rejected when less than 95% of a track length has accumulated since spawn. If spawn is near the start, the following accepted crossing can therefore occur after close to two track lengths from the spawn. The lap counter counts accepted start-line crossings, while signed progress remains unwrapped from the spawn; they measure different things. The results JSON and CSV retain per-episode signed progress and completed laps to make this visible.

For each seed, the winner had no off-track time or blocking and completed the objective. However, among the 4,000 evaluations per run, the observed termination counts were:

| Seed | Objective | Off-track | Blocked | Time limit |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2026 | 824 | 1301 | 73 | 1802 |
| 2027 | 778 | 1530 | 137 | 1555 |
| 2028 | 1312 | 1186 | 13 | 1489 |

The reward audit found and fixed one exploit before collecting this data: the previous on-track uptime and forward-speed bonuses could accrue while forward/reverse oscillations canceled in net track progress. Those bonuses now require positive validated signed progress in the step, with a regression test. No reward weights were tuned to improve the reported scores. The 95% lap threshold also prevents a partial trip from random spawn from being credited as a lap. The full generation table, including reward and physical termination metrics, is in `results/phase4-generation-statistics.csv`; the JSON contains full histories, best networks and paired runs.
